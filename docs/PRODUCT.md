# SilverScope Product Scope

## Positioning
SilverScope is an independent silver-market intelligence terminal. Its value is not a chart alone: it explains how physical inventory, macro conditions, industrial demand and market positioning interact.

## Primary users
- Active silver-market traders
- Macro and commodity investors
- Commodity researchers and analysts

## Product modules
1. Dashboard: composite score, market regime, component drivers and risk context.
2. Signals: directional state, horizon, evidence, confidence, invalidation and change log.
3. Indicators: categorized observations with units, source and timestamps.
4. Alerts: user-defined threshold and event notifications.
5. Research: daily/weekly reports and scenario analysis.
6. Billing: Free, Pro and Ultra entitlements.
7. Admin: source health, ingestion runs, model versions and user entitlements.

## Subscription hypothesis
- Free: overview and selected educational/indicator content.
- Pro (proposed $29/month): full score history, multi-horizon analysis and alerts.
- Ultra (proposed $99/month): scenario research, model evaluation and planned API access.

Prices are hypotheses and require customer validation. Checkout is not implemented.

## Non-goals for MVP
- Brokerage execution or custody
- Personalized portfolio recommendations
- Claims of predictive accuracy without reproducible evaluation
- Unlicensed redistribution of exchange or vendor data
